
# Real Mart Sales Analysis Dashboard (Microsoft Excel)

## Project Overview

The Real Mart Sales Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Excel-based business intelligence project designed to analyze sales performance and provide actionable insights through dynamic visualizations and KPI tracking. The dashboard enables users to monitor sales trends, profitability, customer ratings, branch performance, city-wise revenue, and payment method distribution using an intuitive and user-friendly interface.

## Objectives

* Analyze overall sales performance and profitability.
* Track key business metrics through KPI cards.
* Compare sales across cities, branches, and product categories.
* Visualize monthly sales trends.
* Understand customer purchasing behavior and payment preferences.
* Enable interactive data exploration through filters and slicers.

## Features

* Interactive Slicers for Product Line, City, Branch, and Gender.
* Timeline Filter for date-based analysis.
* Monthly Sales Trend Visualization.
* City-wise Sales Comparison.
* Branch-wise Sales Performance Analysis.
* Product Category Sales and Gross Income Analysis.
* Payment Method Distribution Dashboard.
* Dynamic KPI Summary Cards.

## Business Insights

* Delhi generated the highest sales among all cities.
* Branch A contributed the largest share of total revenue.
* Food and Beverages was the highest-performing product category.
* Cash payments accounted for the majority of customer transactions.
* Monthly sales trends revealed fluctuations in customer demand patterns.
